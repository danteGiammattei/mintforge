Used here: the 32px "scroller" sheets — flat colours (chest-albedo.png, was
wooden-chest_32px_scroller_no-outline_unlit.png) and normal map
(chest-normal.png, was wooden-chest_32px_scroller_normal.png).

Changes made in MintForge, at run time (src/lib/chestArt.js): the four flat
colours are replaced per chest tier (wooden, iron, gilded, obsidian, void),
and each frame is lit with the game's own light rigs from the normal map,
then quantised to six tones.
